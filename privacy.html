Privacy policy

YAT Archive Backup · Updated 28 October 2026

Purpose and operator

YAT Archive Backup is a private-use utility operated by Dmitry Bykov. It stores owner-selected archive backups in the owner's Google Drive and checks their integrity.

Data accessed and stored

The utility processes selected archive content supplied by the owner's backup process, backup filenames, file IDs, folder relationships, file sizes, checksums and verification timestamps. It uploads backup files and verification records and downloads the uploaded files to verify them.

Google OAuth credentials are stored separately on the operator's server to maintain authorized access. They are not published on this site or included in the archive backup produced by this utility.

Google permissions

The utility requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. Access is limited to files created by the application or explicitly made available to it. It does not request full-Drive access.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used to provide backup, verification and recovery functions. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train generalized AI models. Backup data passes between the operator-controlled server and Google Drive. This public website contains no backup files or archive content.

Storage and retention

Backup copies are stored on the operator's server and in the designated Google Drive location. Dated Drive copies are retained until the owner deletes them; this utility does not automatically delete previous backup versions. Temporary files used for verification are removed after processing. Operational records may be retained to verify completed uploads and avoid duplicates.

Control and deletion

The owner can stop the backup service and revoke Google access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself delete existing backups. Drive copies can be deleted in Google Drive; server copies and stored credentials can be removed by the operator.
